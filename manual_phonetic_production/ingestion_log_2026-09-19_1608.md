## Discovery
Accepted `ruthven2023_rtmri_gt`: Ruthven et al. (2023), *Real-time speech MRI datasets with corresponding articulator ground-truth segmentations*, Scientific Data 10:860; public dataset Zenodo v2 DOI 10.5281/zenodo.10046815. This is primary non-singing English speech production from five healthy adults counting 1–10. The source contains 392 rtMRI frames across five series (105, 71, 71, 78, 67), manually created pixel-level GT segmentations, and expert human velopharyngeal-contact labels. The six mask classes are background/head/soft palate/jaw/tongue/vocal tract/tooth space. Four MRI physicists independently labelled contact; majority or expert consensus produced GT. Seven images per series (~10%) were manually re-segmented for intra-rater validation. Archive: `data.zip`, 21.9 MB, MD5 `2c5fb95e6294ecf97204f64128137a96`.

Screened additional candidates. PAVOQUE is already represented in the backlog/ingested fragments and was not duplicated. BELMASK is primary production but discovery evidence did not establish a manual phonetic measurement/annotation layer, so it was not admitted. VoxAngeles has manually audited phone alignments but reuses UCLA Phonetics Lab Archive recordings; only the newly contributed audited annotation/measurement layer can qualify and must remain lineage-isolated. Singing/music hits were excluded.

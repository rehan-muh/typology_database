### IFCASL Corpus — HIGH review (`ifcasl_2016`)
- Primary bilingual French/German learner + native read-speech corpus with about 100 speakers.
- Published corpus description reports word- and phone-level annotation/segmentation with **more than 50% manually corrected data**.
- Project documentation separately states that automatic speech-text alignment was applied first and then French and German speech data were manually checked and annotated by the respective project teams.
- Kept in review rather than core because the current pass did not locate an openly enumerable corpus deposit. Core admission requires isolating manually corrected word/phone tiers from untouched forced-aligned material and recording correction coverage and licensing at file/tier level.

### Berlin Dialogue Corpus (BeDiaCo) v2 — HIGH review (`bediaco_v2_2021`)
- Primary German production corpus with **36 participants**, topic-led and task-led spontaneous dialogues, and read word lists.
- Documentation explicitly states that the corpus contains audio plus Praat TextGrid files and uses a multi-layer annotation architecture.
- Reported annotation layers include diplomatic transliteration, **phonetic segmentation**, filler particles, intonation phrases, dialogue structure, word types, and realization of inflection.
- Kept in review pending current HU media-repository file enumeration, licensing and tier-level provenance checks distinguishing manual phonetic/prosodic annotation from any automatically generated layers.

### 1. Pöhnlein & Kleber 2023 — obstruent-intrinsic f0 and VOT
- source_id: `lmu_poehnlein_kleber_2023_vot_f0`
- DOI: `10.5282/ubm/data.384`
- license: CC BY 4.0
- primary German/West Central Bavarian production data.
- Public deposit contains three analysis scripts and three data files: `CF0_trackdata.txt` (~7 MB), `CF0_VOT_correlation.Rdata` (~17 kB), `VOT_landmarks.txt` (~356 kB), plus three R scripts.
- `VOT_landmarks.txt` was opened directly. It contains a header plus 1,723 exposed observation rows. Original columns are: `session var_age bundle word repetition c.type POA position freq word.start word.end vow.start vow.end clos.start clos.end VOT.end`.
- Source-faithful variable dictionary/schema was written for all 16 fields. Landmark values are preserved as source-supplied milliseconds; no durations, IPA, or context were invented.
- The table is not promoted to accepted/core yet because the exact manual creation/correction status of these landmarks still requires verification from the associated methods/paper or scripts. This is intentionally conservative despite the data being exceptionally suitable for the database.
- The large `CF0_trackdata.txt` endpoint was confirmed public but exceeds the web parser's direct-content limit; it remains a priority for programmatic download/parse. It must be retained as a full trajectory, not midpoint-reduced.

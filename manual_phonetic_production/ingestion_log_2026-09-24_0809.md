## Discovery

### SAIT-EMA (review)
Fresh Scientific Data/Zenodo candidate: primary Mandarin/L2 Mandarin EMA + synchronized acoustic speech from 18 adults (9F/9M; 12 native Mandarin, 6 L2 from Vietnamese, Peruvian, Russian, Mexican backgrounds). AG501 EMA sampled at 250 Hz; audio 48 kHz/16-bit/mono. The corpus has 575 monosyllables + 284 disyllables + 103 sentences = 962 target items per speaker. During collection, misread tokens were re-recorded and logged; post-recording, misaligned tokens and unintended repetitions were manually screened and removed, yielding one verified token per target. However, supplied syllable initial/final TextGrids are Montreal Forced Aligner output and the authors explicitly warn that high-precision landmarks may require manual verification/refinement. Therefore: preserve raw EMA/audio as a potentially eligible exhaustively human-screened primary-production layer, but DO NOT admit the automatic TextGrid boundaries. Keep source in review pending a policy decision/verification that the final raw trajectory tokens meet the database's human-verification threshold and pending object-level Zenodo enumeration.

Primary references: DOI 10.1038/s41597-026-07237-9; data DOI 10.5281/zenodo.17506802; code https://github.com/Altamen/SAIT-EMA.

### IFCASL (review)
Re-screened the French-German bilingual learner corpus. About 100 speakers; word- and phone-level segmentation; >50% of data manually corrected; paper reports inter-annotator agreement. This is highly likely to contribute a large eligible layer, but only if manually corrected objects/intervals can be isolated from remaining forced alignment. Kept in review rather than admitting the whole corpus.

Primary reference: https://aclanthology.org/L16-1212/.

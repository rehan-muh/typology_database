## Discovery

### SAIT-EMA (`sait_ema_2025_2026`) — REVIEW

New 2026 Scientific Data / Zenodo source: 18 Mandarin native/L2 speakers, AG501 EMA at 250 Hz and synchronized 48-kHz audio. Primary recordings were manually screened against session logs to remove misaligned tokens and unintended repetitions, but the distributed syllable initial/final TextGrids are MFA-generated and the paper explicitly warns of temporal offsets and recommends manual verification/refinement for high-precision landmarks. Because human QA applies to the token set rather than verified phonetic boundaries, this is retained in review rather than admitted to the manual core.

### Parker sonority data (`parker_sonority_2005_2008_2019`) — REVIEW

Mendeley Data version 6 supplies the primary English/Spanish WAV recordings used for Parker's Journal of Phonetics sonority study and explicitly states that the WAVs were manually segmented and analyzed in Praat 4.3.17. The deposit is CC BY 4.0. Current deposit-level evidence advertises raw WAVs rather than the actual manual segmentation/derived-measurement tables, so it is queued for deeper file enumeration rather than admitted on documentation alone.

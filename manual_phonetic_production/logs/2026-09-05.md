# Manual phonetic production crawl — 2026-09-05

## Scope
Fresh searches covered OSF, Zenodo, GitHub and recent phonetics/sociophonetics literature, with emphasis on Journal of Phonetics, JIPA, Language and Speech and open repository records. Eligibility remained restricted to primary production recordings whose relevant segment boundaries, landmarks, labels or measurements were manual or exhaustively human-corrected/verified.

## Backlog conversion progress

### PAVOQUE
PAVOQUE remains accepted. The public repository documentation states that its phonetic segments are **manually corrected**, labels are SAMPA, and segment end times are supplied relative to utterance starts. The corpus is primary German expressive/read speech from one speaker and is licensed CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0.

Added `scripts/pavoque_to_canonical.py` to convert all style YAML files directly to:

- `coarticulation_tokens.csv`, with globally namespaced `pavoque::Stefan_Roettig`, production language, style/task covariates, target timing and adjacent-phone context;
- `coarticulation_events.csv`, with canonical `SEGMENT_ONSET` and `SEGMENT_OFFSET` events anchored to the manually corrected phone boundaries.

The converter uses a conservative documented German SAMPA-to-IPA mapping. Unmapped source labels are never guessed: IPA remains blank, the source label is retained in `source_annotation_id`, and the row receives `UNMAPPED_SOURCE_PHONE`. No trajectory table is fabricated; acoustic trajectories remain `REEXTRACT` from source audio. The ingestion queue was updated accordingly. A derived batch has not yet been committed in this crawl, so no validator success is claimed here; every generated batch must be passed through `scripts/validate_coarticulation.py` before inclusion.

## Fresh candidate review

### Ostrand & Chodroff 2021 — *Journal of Phonetics* 88:101074
- URL: https://osf.io/ks9zx
- DOI: 10.1016/j.wocn.2021.101074
- Primary recordings: yes.
- Annotation evidence: recordings were manually segmented at the utterance level, then phone-level canonical pronunciations were aligned with Montreal Forced Aligner; the paper does not report exhaustive manual correction of the phone boundaries used for segment-level acoustic measurements.
- Decision: **remain excluded** (`FORCED_ALIGNMENT_UNVERIFIED`). This source was already correctly represented in `data/registry/exclusions.csv`; no duplicate record was added.

### Wood 2025 — Chichicastenango K’iche’ vowel production, JIPA
- DOI: 10.1017/S0025100325100698
- Primary controlled production experiment: yes; eleven speakers are described in the associated material.
- Public material located in this crawl: article and supplementary audio examples, but not a complete public token-level dataset with verified boundary/measurement provenance suitable for canonical ingestion.
- Decision: **not accepted in this crawl**; no capability row added because full reusable source files satisfying the ingestion contract were not located.

### French OSCE Dialogue Dataset 2026 — Zenodo
- DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.20719833 / record family 20627432
- Primary recordings: yes; restricted-access clinical dialogue recordings.
- Manual material: corrected orthographic transcriptions for a subset, not segment/phone boundaries or target acoustic landmarks.
- Decision: **not supported for the ten target analyses** under the current annotation tier; no accepted coarticulation source added.

### UrduSpeech 2026
- Large Urdu corpus with a manually corrected benchmark subset was surfaced, but the available description does not establish manually corrected segment boundaries/landmarks for the ten target analyses and describes a broad curation pipeline rather than the required segment-level production annotations.
- Decision: **not accepted in this crawl** pending evidence that the relevant phonetic tier is manually created or exhaustively corrected and that recordings satisfy the primary-collection rule.

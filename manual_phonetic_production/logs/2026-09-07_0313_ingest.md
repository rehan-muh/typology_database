## Accepted

### VoxAngeles manual layer (Chodroff et al. 2024)
- Accepted only the **new manually corrected/audited annotation layer** over inherited UCLA/CMU recordings; inherited recordings are lineage-linked and must not be counted as a new recording corpus.
- Upstream README explicitly separates `data/audited_aligned/` (manually corrected phone-level alignments + audited phonetic transcriptions) from `data/unaudited/` (unreviewed force alignments).
- Audited portion currently covers **95 languages across 21 language families**.
- New manual layer: phone boundaries, phone labels/transcriptions, word linkage/context.
- Derived public measurements: phone/word durations, vowel F0, vowel F1/F2/F3, file-level mean F0.
- Decile trajectory tables are preserved as trajectories; quartile tables are overlapping alternate representations and are not treated as independent tokens.
- Public license reported by upstream: CC BY-NC 4.0.

The source extraction scripts were inspected to recover exact semantics rather than guessing column meanings. `getDurations_voxangeles.praat` exports `lang,file,word,phone,prec,foll,int,pstart,pend,wstart,wend`. `getf0_voxangeles.praat` samples F0 at ten equally spaced positions across each audited vowel interval using Praat Pitch (75–500 Hz). `getFormants_voxangeles.praat` exports F1–F3 at ten equally spaced positions using Burg formant tracking; its source script chooses a 5000- vs 5500-Hz formant ceiling from recording mean F0. A semantic variable dictionary was committed with original/source and standardized fields kept separate.

A reproducible ingester, `scripts/ingest_voxangeles_manual_layer.py`, was added. It downloads all six TSVs, preserves source-faithful copies, computes SHA-256, inventories every table/column, namespaces recording/token IDs, derives duration without overwriting boundaries, and creates long F0/F1/F2/F3 trajectory tables with original sample variable plus normalized time. It deliberately does not copy inherited UCLA audio.

### Observation-count policy

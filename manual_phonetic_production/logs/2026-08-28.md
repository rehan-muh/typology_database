# Phonetic production crawl — 2026-08-28

## Added to accepted/source registry

1. **Mitra & Dutta (2023), Bengali–English bilingual vowel production** — OSF `dsb2x`; 10 speakers and 3,200 target tokens. The paper explicitly states that recordings were manually segmented and annotated in Praat before F1/F2 extraction at five points. Added as `INCLUDE_MEASUREMENTS`; audio/TextGrid availability remains to be checked file-by-file.
2. **Cohn & Zellou (2023), California-English clear/casual/fast-clear speech** — OSF `n3fzj`; 27 speakers, 2,698 utterances annotated and 2,615 retained. Every analyzed target-vowel boundary was hand-corrected after MFA using acoustic criteria. Added as `INCLUDE_MEASUREMENTS`.
3. **L2-ARCTIC manual subset** — 24 non-native English speakers. Only the 3,599 manually examined utterances qualify; documentation reports corrected word/phone boundaries plus substitution/deletion/addition tags. The remaining automatically aligned corpus is not treated as manual ground truth.

## Added exclusion

- **SpiCE phone/word tiers** — primary recordings but excluded because documentation explicitly says MFA phone/word tiers received only coarse inspection and no manual correction. Hand-corrected orthographic transcription is insufficient for phone-boundary inclusion.

## Added to review queue

- **IFCASL** — >50% manually corrected word/phone segmentation across roughly 100 French/German native and learner speakers; need a machine-identifiable manual-only subset and license/access verification.
- **SFB 632 100 sentences** — Georgian, German, Prinmi and Teribe production resources have manual phonetic/prosodic annotation; need audio/time-alignment/provenance/license verification.
- **Rhapsodie spoken French** — aligned phoneme/syllable/word annotation and manually corrected F0 appear highly useful, but primary-vs-reused recording lineage must be resolved before inclusion.

## Evidence checked

- Mitra & Dutta paper: methods explicitly report `10 × 20 × 2 × 2 × 4 = 3200` target tokens and manual Praat segmentation; data availability points to OSF DOI `10.17605/OSF.IO/DSB2X`.
- Cohn & Zellou paper: methods report trained-RA review of all utterances, MFA followed by target-vowel hand correction, and OSF DOI `10.17605/OSF.IO/N3FZJ`.
- L2-ARCTIC corpus documentation: 3,599 utterances manually examined, with 14,098 substitutions, 3,420 deletions and 1,092 additions.
- SpiCE documentation: explicitly states word/phone forced alignment was only coarsely inspected and not manually corrected.

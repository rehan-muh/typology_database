# Discovery/adjudication log — 2026-08-27

Initial repository bootstrap.

## Accepted or conditionally accepted
- Twi vowel-harmony production dataset (OSF): manual target-vowel annotation.
- SwehVd (OSF): forced-alignment proposals with all relevant vowel boundaries manually corrected.
- CCOST (Zenodo): ASR/forced alignment followed by trained-native-speaker manual correction.
- PAVOQUE (GitHub): manually corrected phone segmentation.
- Ohio State Stories: every target-vowel onset/offset hand-corrected.
- Schertz et al. imitation experiment (OSF): manually placed burst, voicing-onset and vowel-end landmarks.
- Oschkinat et al. temporal feedback experiment (OSF): automatically proposed segment durations hand-corrected in Praat.
- Jingju a cappella collection: manually segmented, but retain only newly recorded primary subset.
- MRPL2: manually annotated; files currently restricted.

## Excluded
- VoxAngeles: secondary UCLA archive despite manual correction.
- Ostrand & Chodroff phone-level measures: MFA phone alignment without exhaustive reported phone-boundary correction.
- Neural acoustic distance: Speech Accent Archive reuse.
- COVYT / PodcastFillers: third-party social-media/podcast audio, not primary collection.
- Buckeye-derived measurement repositories: secondary corpus reuse.
- Clopper et al. 2023 derivative analysis: de-duplicated to original Ohio State Stories source.

## High-priority next checks
AutoVOT manual training ground truth; Shaw & Kawahara EMA/acoustic OSF; Welby et al. Journal of Phonetics production data; Bahdini Kurdish VOT; recent Journal of Phonetics manual-aspiration datasets.

## Discovery

### Accepted: `coretta_italian_vowel_2018_2026`

Fresh discovery identified the public `coretta2018itaegg` data package accompanying work on Northwestern Italian vowel duration/height. The source clears the manual-verification criterion: source documentation/paper methods describe SPPAS alignment followed by manual correction in Praat, and FastTrack formant tracks were manually checked with tracking settings adjusted where necessary.

Public package audit:

- 19 speakers.
- `ita_egg.rda`: 3,268 observations × 52 variables.
- `formants.rda`: 3,053 observations × 38 variables; overlapping/derived lineage with `ita_egg`, therefore not independently counted.
- Raw source files identified and checksum-addressed at GitHub blob level: `measurements.csv` (727,044 B), `stimuli.csv` (14,927 B), `formants-ids.csv` (58,783 B), `words.csv` (367 B).
- `ita_egg` exposes sentence/word/V1/C2/V2/voice/release landmarks, C1/V1/C2 identities and place coding, vowel duration, closure and voicing timing, VOT, F0, speech rate, FastTrack cutoff, and F1/F2/F3 in five successive 20%-wide vowel windows.
- License: CC-BY-4.0.

Committed:

- accepted-source increment;
- six-file manifest with lineage/deduplication notes;
- source-specific 52-variable dictionary;
- reproducible `scripts/ingest_coretta_italian_vowels.py` converter.

The converter preserves the four public source CSVs unchanged, namespaces speaker/token IDs, emits linked token/landmark/temporal/source-derived tables, and converts F1–F3 to long trajectories while retaining original 20%-wide windows. Normalized times 0.1/0.3/0.5/0.7/0.9 are explicitly marked as derived window centers rather than replacements for original time windows. No IPA is inferred.

Two source-documentation inconsistencies were retained and flagged rather than silently repaired: the Rd description for `voice_off` repeats “voice onset”, although source formulas use it as the offset; and the Rd text describes `c2_clos_duration` as C1 closure while the source formula is `(c2_rel-c2_ons)*1000`, i.e. C2 closure.

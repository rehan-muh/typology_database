## Accepted

### Cox et al. (2023) — Danish infant-directed/adult-directed speech
- Publication DOI: 10.1111/cdev.13950
- OSF: https://osf.io/ywf9m/
- Language: Danish
- Primary collection: yes; 26 caregivers recorded in Aarhus, Denmark (2016–2018)
- Manual evidence: methods explicitly report manual Praat segmentation of the onset and offset of **9,267 vowels**.
- Measurements: vowel duration and formants; public analysis data/code on OSF.
- Audio/video: explicitly **not public** according to the Data Availability statement, therefore no raw recordings are copied into this repository.
- Decision: `INCLUDE_MEASUREMENTS`.

### Chang, Chien & Lu (2026) — Mandarin learners' English vowel imitation
- Publication DOI: 10.1177/02676583261461946
- OSF: https://osf.io/43dyh/
- Languages: Mandarin; L2 English
- Primary collection: yes; 25 Mandarin-speaking participants recorded individually in a sound-attenuated booth.
- Scale: 180 imitation trials/participant = **4,500 planned production trials**.
- Manual evidence: all recorded imitations manually annotated in Praat by three phonetically trained RAs; 12% common subset annotated by all three.
- Reliability reported: Krippendorff alpha F1=.92, F2=.80, duration=.86.
- Measurements: vowel duration and midpoint F1/F2; data and analysis scripts publicly deposited on OSF.
- Decision: `INCLUDE_MEASUREMENTS` pending any later file-level expansion to raw annotation/audio if OSF licensing and contents permit.

## Excluded

### Geutjes, Junge & Chen (2025) — Dutch intonational phrase boundaries
- DOI: 10.1017/S0025100325100741
- Annotation provenance is strong: pre-boundary syllables and pauses manually segmented in Praat.
- However, the article states that data/materials are available **on request** through Yoda rather than as an openly downloadable public dataset.
- Decision: `NOT_PUBLICLY_ACCESSIBLE` under the project's present inclusion policy.

## Review queue

### Wang et al. (2025) — Central Australian Aboriginal English monophthongs
- DOI: 10.1111/weng.70003
- Six adult female L1 CAAE speakers; conversational primary recordings.
- **1,836** monophthong tokens manually annotated with explicit onset/offset criteria.
- Duration and F1–F4 extracted at 10/30/50/70/90% of vowel duration.
- The crawl verified the article and ANU publication record but did not locate an openly downloadable token-level data/annotation deposit.
- Keep in review until public accessibility is established.

## Discovery notes

Searches covered OSF-linked studies, Zenodo/manual-segmentation queries, Journal of Phonetics, Laboratory Phonology, JIPA, JSLHR, and broader current phonetics/sociophonetics literature. Several results were deliberately not registered because they were perception-only, automatic-validation studies rather than reusable phonetic production annotations, or lacked a public data deposit.

Their records are therefore admitted at the measurement layer with provenance retained. Future runs should inspect OSF file inventories and normalize token-level tables when accessible.

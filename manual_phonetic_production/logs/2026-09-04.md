## Accepted

### Jingju a Cappella Recordings Collection — primary manually segmented subset

Zenodo DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.6536490

The JaCRC is a mixed-origin collection, so admission is explicitly subset-scoped. Zenodo documents 314 vocal recordings and manual segmentation for 217 vocal recordings. The annotation documentation states that all segmentation was performed manually in Praat; 142 recordings have phrase/syllable segmentation, 81 of these additionally have phoneme segmentation, and student recordings have phrase/syllable/phoneme segmentation. Phoneme labels use X-SAMPA. The same source separately identifies 53 recordings reused from the Singing Voice Audio Dataset and 15 commercial-source items. Those reused/commercial items are not admissible under the primary-recording criterion and must never be treated as part of the accepted subset. Newly created JaCRC recordings are CC BY 4.0.

Decision: INCLUDE_SPECIALIZED, with primary-recording subset restriction.

### Carignan 2024 earbuds/nasalance validation

Article DOI: 10.1121/10.0028122
OSF: https://osf.io/3wq9t/

Primary controlled American-English production from the author as sole participant. WebMAUS supplied initial word/phone segmentation, after which both word- and phone-level boundaries were manually corrected by the author. The JASA Data Availability statement specifies that the data and R code needed to recreate the results are publicly available on OSF. This clears the exhaustive-human-correction rule. Admission is conservative at the derived-measurement layer pending direct inventory of whether raw audio and corrected TextGrids are also exposed in the OSF project.

Decision: INCLUDE_MEASUREMENTS.

## Review queue

### Nieber, Kachel & Scharinger 2026 — Acoustic representations of different dimensions of the gendered self

DOI: 10.1016/j.wocn.2026.101512

Primary semi-spontaneous German speech from 53 speakers (52 analyzed). The paper reports manual segmentation/annotation in Praat at picture, carrier-word, and target-sound levels and states that the dataset, Praat scripts, and R code are available in an OSF repository. The exact OSF project identifier and released file layers were not independently resolved during this crawl, so it remains a high-priority review candidate rather than being admitted on article wording alone.

# Phonetic production data crawl — 2026-08-29

## Added to accepted registry

### xi_li_prieto_2024_l2_vowels
- Publication: Xi, Li & Prieto (2024), *Language Learning*, DOI 10.1111/lang.12647.
- Repository: OSF https://osf.io/mnfxb/
- Primary production: Catalan-Spanish bilingual learners producing L2 English in paragraph reading, picture naming, and word imitation.
- Scale: 99 participants; 10,054 recorded production items; 9,837 retained target-vowel items after exclusions.
- Manual provenance: the first author manually annotated target /ae/ and /uh/ in Praat. F1/F2 were then scripted at vowel midpoint and abnormal values were manually checked/corrected.
- Decision: INCLUDE_MEASUREMENTS.

### parker_sonority_2008_2019
- Publication: Parker (2008), *Journal of Phonetics*, related article DOI 10.1016/j.wocn.2007.09.003.
- Repository/data DOI: Mendeley Data https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/5jcwt7rffw/6 ; 10.17632/5jcwt7rffw.6.
- Primary production: English speakers recorded in Ukarumpa, Papua New Guinea, and Spanish speakers recorded in Lima, Peru, in 2005.
- Manual provenance: repository explicitly states the WAV files were manually segmented and analyzed in Praat 4.3.17.
- Access/license: public; CC BY 4.0.
- Decision: INCLUDE. This is especially valuable because raw WAV recordings are openly available and linked to explicit manual segmentation provenance.

### turead_oral_reading_2023
- Publication: Acartürk et al. (2023/2024), *Behavior Research Methods*, DOI 10.3758/s13428-023-02120-6.
- Repository: OSF https://osf.io/w53cz/
- Primary production: Turkish oral reading within a 196-participant reading experiment.
- Manual provenance: target-word articulation onset and offset were manually annotated in ELAN by listening to the recordings and identifying waveform onsets/offsets; 92.39% of audio-recording annotations were controlled and refined by a second annotator.
- Decision: INCLUDE_MEASUREMENTS. The OSF Data Availability statement explicitly covers dataset tables; raw audio/EAF redistribution is not assumed until separately verified.

## Added to review queue

### spanish_vowel_reduction_manual_validation_subset
A recent study reports 1,043 Spanish vowels whose boundaries were manually assigned for forced-alignment validation, with duration and F1-F3 recomputed from those human boundaries. Only this manual subset could qualify. Need to verify public token-level deposit and primary recording provenance before admission.

### eastern_andalusian_harmony_2026
A 2026 JIPA study reports manual Praat segmentation of stressed/pre-tonic vowels followed by scripted F1/F2/F0/F3 extraction. Need to locate a public data repository/supplement and verify license before admission.

## Deliberate non-admissions
- Resources where manual correction covered only a validation subset were not generalized to the automatically aligned remainder.
- Studies with explicit manual segmentation but no located public token-level repository were placed in review rather than accepted.
- Search hits concerning linguistic annotation without phonetic-production timing/measurement were ignored.

# Phonetic data crawl — 2026-08-31

## Outcome

No new source was admitted to the core registry today. The strict primary-data requirement rejected two otherwise high-quality manually segmented datasets and added three promising resources to the adjudication queue. Cumulative status after this run: 17 accepted, 13 excluded, 15 review candidates.

## New exclusions

### Hiemstra & Sadakata (2024), speech-to-song vowel space
- Publication: https://doi.org/10.1177/10298649231224786
- Manual evidence: all analyzed recordings were manually segmented in Praat with word, phoneme, and vowel tiers.
- Exclusion: the speech came from audiobooks and existing UvA/Tierney speech-to-song stimulus collections, so it fails the primary-recording rule despite excellent annotation provenance.

### Shi, Gu & Vigliocco (2023), child-directed prosody
- Publication: https://doi.org/10.1111/desc.13357
- Public analysis data: https://osf.io/ykcvf/
- Manual evidence: MAUS word alignments were manually corrected by an expert; all 6,658 target-word boundaries were checked/corrected in Praat; 6,529 clean target referents entered analysis.
- Exclusion: recordings are explicitly drawn from the pre-existing ECOLANG corpus. Under the current policy, this is a secondary corpus analysis rather than a new primary collection.

## New review candidates

### Fagniart et al. (2025), French nasal/oral vowels in children
- Publication: https://doi.org/10.1044/2024_JSLHR-24-00083
- Scale: 38 children; 1,037 sentences; 2,074 vowel productions.
- Provenance: all 2,074 vowels manually segmented/annotated in Praat; abnormal formant values manually inspected/corrected; rich duration, F1-F3 and nasality measures.
- Blocker: no openly downloadable token-level acoustic/annotation deposit was verified in this crawl.

### RescueSpeech
- Repository: https://zenodo.org/records/8077622
- Scale: 2,412 clean utterances, 1h36m, 26 speakers; 4-GB open archive.
- Primary collection: authentic German recordings from simulated search-and-rescue exercises.
- Provenance: Zenodo describes recordings as manually annotated.
- Blocker: public documentation does not specify whether manual annotation includes temporally aligned word/phone boundaries or only utterance transcripts/labels. Archive-level inspection is needed before admission.

### Karl Eberhards Corpus (KEC)
- Discovery page: https://uni-tuebingen.de/en/faculties/faculty-of-humanities/departments/modern-languages/department-of-linguistics/chairs/quantitative-linguistics/corpora/
- Primary collection: 40 spontaneous southern-German dialogues; articulography for 20 speakers.
- Provenance: manually corrected word boundaries; segment boundaries are described as automatically aligned.
- Blocker: verify download/license and cleanly isolate the human-corrected word-level layer. Automatic segment boundaries must remain excluded unless later evidence shows exhaustive human correction.

## Search notes

The crawl also rechecked IFCASL. It remains in review: the corpus was specifically designed/recorded by the IFCASL project and >50% of its word/phone segmentation was manually corrected, but the public website currently exposes sample recordings/annotations rather than a clearly downloadable full manual-only subset. The 2026 ROG Slovenian corpus was not admitted because it is explicitly built on the pre-existing SST/GOS 2 resources.

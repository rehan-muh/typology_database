## Deep backlog ingestion: Tang, Parrell & Niziolek 2022

A new reproducible deep-audit script, `scripts/audit_tang_mat.py`, recursively enumerates OSF node `stjc9`, parses MATLAB structures, records source checksums and object paths, and identifies source-faithful candidate tables without imposing guessed variable semantics.

Fresh audit results:

- 84 file-listing rows representing **82 unique OSF paths** (the API returned duplicate entries for the `sp239` dataVals/expt pair).
- **54 unique MATLAB paths**, including **24 unique participant `dataVals.mat` files** and **24 `expt.mat` files**.
- The 24 participant dataVals files contain exactly **13,000 trial structures**: 22 speakers with 540 trial structures, `sp166` with 640, and `sp169` with 480.
- Every audited `dataVals` trial structure exposes the same source fields: `f0`, `f1`, `f2`, `int`, `pitch_taxis`, `ftrack_taxis`, `ampl_taxis`, `dur`, `word`, `vowel`, `color`, `cond`, `token`, `bExcl`.
- F1 and F2 are stored as within-token vectors paired with `ftrack_taxis`; the first 1,200 fully profiled trials had a median of 109 F1/F2 samples per token and a range from 0 to 172 samples. This sampling summary is descriptive of the audited profile subset and is not silently generalized to unprofiled trials.
- F0/intensity have their own much denser time axes (`pitch_taxis`, `ampl_taxis`). Units are deliberately left source-specific where the code/documentation has not yet been resolved.
- Matching experiment structures expose trial labels/configuration including `allConds`, `listConds`, `allWords`, `listWords`, `allVowels`, `listVowels`, `allColors`, `listColors`, `shiftScaleFact`, `shiftMags`, `shiftAngles`; Audapter parameter structures include `pertf1` and `pertf2`.
- A persistent namespaced participant inventory is now stored in `data/warehouse/tang_2022_mat_participant_inventory.csv`.
- A source-faithful MAT field dictionary is now stored in `data/warehouse/tang_2022_mat_field_dictionary.csv`.
- Raw participant arrays are **not** mirrored into GitHub because public download has not yet been treated as permission to redistribute the underlying measurements. The audit script can reproduce the structured extraction from authoritative OSF files.

Important distinction: the previously ingested 1,344 Tang CSV rows are summary/regression tables. They must not be confused with the 13,000 participant trial structures now identified in the MAT layer.

## New accepted source: TaL corpus manual segment subset

Added `tal_corpus_2021_manual_segments`.

- Primary synchronized acoustic waveform, ultrasound tongue imaging and lip video from 82 native English speakers (TaL1 + TaL80).
- UltraSuite explicitly documents manual segmentation of long spontaneous utterances into shorter, typically 5–10 s intervals with start/end times and transcription.
- Only that explicitly manual segment layer is admitted as manually grounded annotation. No phone boundary is inferred to be manual.
- Bulk synchronized data remain authoritative external references rather than being mirrored (~49 GB TaL1 + ~498 GB TaL80).
- Added `data/warehouse/tal_corpus_2021_documented_variable_dictionary.csv` covering manual segment timing/transcription and synchronized waveform/ULT/PARAM/video/sync/prompt streams.

## New accepted annotation layer: UltraSuite UXTD/UXSSD/UPX labels

Added `ultrasuite_manual_labels_2018`, linked to the underlying UltraSuite primary recordings rather than counted as independent audio.

- DataShare DOI `10.7488/ds/2429` contains separate UXTD, UXSSD and UPX label archives plus README.
- UXTD/UXSSD documentation states that the reference subset was manually revised at **speaker level for 60 utterances** and **word level for 199 utterances** by one annotator.
- UltraSuite explicitly warns that supplied phone labels are force-aligned and **not entirely manually revised**; these automatic/constrained phone tiers are retained separately and excluded from the manually grounded phone-boundary core.
- Speech-language-therapist labels are manual where available, including clinical UPX material.
- Added `data/warehouse/ultrasuite_manual_labels_2018_documented_variable_dictionary.csv` that explicitly separates manual reference/SLT tiers from automatic tiers.

## New accepted measurement source: Li et al. 2026 L2 Mandarin vowels

Added `li_mcallister_shiller_tian_grigos_2026` from OSF `9qt5j`.

- 40 English-speaking adult late learners of Mandarin produced repeated Mandarin /u/ and /y/ tokens; the study also contains English /ae/, /ɑ/ and novel-vowel production tasks used as somatosensory measures.
- Praat intensity-based vowel onset/offsets were automatically proposed, but the experimenter visually inspected the boundaries and manually corrected them when needed.
- Formant-track alignment to spectrographic energy was visually inspected with speaker/vowel-specific LPC settings; F1/F2 outliers were visually inspected and manually corrected.
- The public OSF deposit is recorded for file-level ingestion in the next backlog stage.
- Added `data/warehouse/li_mcallister_shiller_tian_grigos_2026_documented_variable_dictionary.csv`, retaining production F1/F2 and boundary variables while representing auditory/somatosensory measures as linked covariates rather than production outcomes.

## Explicit exclusion

- Repository: https://zenodo.org/records/18714702
- The released corpus contains >3M French oral-vowel observations with duration, F1-F5 and F0.
- Zenodo documentation explicitly states that phonetic annotations were generated by Whisper followed by Montreal Forced Aligner.
- No exhaustive human correction of the relevant vowel boundaries/labels is documented.
- Decision: `EXCLUDE_UNVERIFIED_FORCED_ALIGNMENT` despite the source's large size and acoustic richness.

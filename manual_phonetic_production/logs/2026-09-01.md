## Accepted

### Chaturvedi & Shaw (2025), *Phonetic trace effects in experimentally-induced vowel errors*
- Publication DOI: https://doi.org/10.1080/02643294.2025.2573172
- Data: https://osf.io/x8akq/ (OSF DOI 10.17605/OSF.IO/X8AKQ)
- Primary production: yes; Yale sound-attenuated-booth recordings from American-English speakers.
- Speakers: 14 recruited; 12 retained for main analysis.
- Token scale: 5,683 vowel tokens before quality exclusions; 5,439 retained after laughter/formant-quality exclusions; 3,655 critical tokens and 1,784 control tokens.
- Annotation provenance: MFA was used only for initial segmentation. The paper states that all audio segmentation was manually corrected. Praat formant estimates were then hand-corrected using a Matlab interface displaying estimates against the spectrogram; all formant measurements were manually checked/corrected before classification.
- Measurements: vowel boundaries, duration, midpoint F1/F2, vowel/error classification.
- Decision: INCLUDE_MEASUREMENTS. The explicit public OSF data-availability statement plus exhaustive manual correction satisfies the core rule. Raw-audio/annotation-file availability should still be harvested at file level before redistribution.

## Added to review

### Rathcke et al. (2024), JASA P-center study
Manual English vowel-onset/syllable annotation and Japanese native-phonetician annotation subsequently checked/corrected by the first author. OSF exists, but this crawl could verify only example annotations/sound materials, not a complete downloadable token-level annotation release.

### Strycharczuk et al. (2025), Northern Anglo-English ultrasound vowels
Forty primary laboratory speakers; 1,927 lax-vowel productions (1,857 analyzed). MFA acoustic boundaries were checked and manually corrected. Kept in review until a public primary-data/annotation repository is located.

### Friedrichs et al. (2026), multimodal German EMA+EEG+audio dataset
Public CC-BY-4.0 SWISSUbase release with 29 speakers and approximately 17 h of EMA plus audio, EEG, and anatomy. However, the documented event/articulatory onsets are algorithmically derived from TTLs and EMA velocities; manual phonetic landmark verification has not yet been established.

### mngu0 manual prompt layer
Primary British-English multimodal articulatory corpus. JASA documentation explicitly states that acoustic speech was manually segmented into prompts, and the corpus distributes segmentation data. Fine-grained phone-label provenance and current license/registration terms still need verification before core admission.

## Important exclusions/near-misses checked

- DoReCo-derived final-lengthening work remains outside the core: word boundaries were manually corrected, but refined segment alignments were generated automatically; the underlying data are also an existing corpus rather than primary recordings for the paper.
- Several papers with manual segmentation but no verifiable public dataset were not admitted.
- The 2026 UZH multimodal dataset was not admitted merely because it is large/open; the strict human-grounding criterion remains controlling.

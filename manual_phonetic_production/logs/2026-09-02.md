## Accepted

### Indian Stroke Speech Corpus
- Repository: https://zenodo.org/records/15675907
- DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.15675906
- Publication: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-40155-9
- Language: Telugu
- Speakers: 50 stroke patients + 50 healthy controls
- Tasks: five sustained vowels (twice each), plosive-vowel words, short phonetically rich sentence reading
- Primary collection: YES. Recordings were collected in the clinical environment at AIIMS Mangalagiri.
- Manual provenance: PASS. The Scientific Reports methods explicitly state that raw audio was manually segmented into independent utterances of each vowel category and that sentence recordings were similarly segmented.
- Public access: PASS. The paper's Data Availability statement gives the Zenodo corpus DOI/record.
- Lineage: no pre-existing speech database dependency identified.
- License: not independently verified in this crawl; do not redistribute raw audio until repository rights are checked.
- Extraction status: registry admission only. The web-accessible evidence verifies the corpus and provenance, but a file-level Zenodo listing could not be reliably retrieved during this run; no raw audio was copied.

## Accepted / lineage-resolved

### voxangeles_2024
Accepted **only for the genuinely new, isolatable manual annotation layer and measurements derived from it**. The underlying UCLA Phonetics Lab Archive / CMU re-release recordings are secondary and must not be counted as new recordings. VoxAngeles begins from 95-language word-level material, force-aligns phones, then manually adjusts/audits phone alignments and transcriptions. Upstream explicitly separates `data/audited_aligned` from `data/unaudited`; the latter is excluded from the manual core.

Current published scope: 95 audited languages across 21 Glottolog families. License: CC-BY-NC 4.0. Public upstream repository: https://github.com/pacscilab/voxangeles . Publication: Chodroff, Pažon, Baker & Moran 2024, *Phonetic Segmentation of the UCLA Phonetics Lab Archive*, LREC-COLING 2024.

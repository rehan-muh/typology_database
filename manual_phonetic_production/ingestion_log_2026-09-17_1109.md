### UltraSuite

Authoritative documentation establishes five relevant primary non-singing production collections: UXTD (58 typically developing children), UXSSD (8 children with speech sound disorders), UltraPhonix/UPX (20 children with speech sound disorders), Cleft (29 consented children with cleft lip/palate), and UX2020 (37 consented children with speech sound disorders). Modalities include synchronized or paired WAV speech and raw ultrasound plus acquisition parameters/prompts. Manual `slt_labels` are available when present. UXTD/UXSSD additionally have manually revised reference labels: 60 utterances at speaker level and 199 utterances at word level, revised by one annotator. Their phone reference tier is NOT fully manually revised: it is force-aligned within manually revised word boundaries, so it must not be represented as manual phone segmentation.

Public access is via rsync. Core archive sizes documented upstream: UXTD 82 GB; UXSSD 110 GB; UPX 191 GB; Cleft 85 GB; UX2020 91 GB. Mixed label archive for UXTD/UXSSD/UPX is ~80 MB. Cleft labels are ~316 KB. These annotation archives are the next ingestion target; raw audio/ultrasound should not be committed to GitHub.

Cleft and UX2020 have failed hardware synchronization according to upstream documentation; this caveat must propagate to any articulatory/acoustic linkage. Do not silently use automatically predicted synchronization as manual timing.

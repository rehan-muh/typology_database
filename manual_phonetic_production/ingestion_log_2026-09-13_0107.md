## Fresh discovery

### LoKiS — Longitudinal Study of Kid's Speech

Added to HIGH review as `lokis_child_speech`.

Evidence gathered this run:

- Current Friedrich Schiller University Jena corpus page describes six longitudinal waves (2020–2025), beginning with 62 children aged 6–7 and continuing through age 11–12.
- Production material includes picture naming, counting, sentence repetition and picture description.
- The corpus page states that all recordings are segmented and annotated and that words/segments are searchable.
- Funk & Simpson (2023, JSLHR, DOI 10.1044/2023_JSLHR-22-00682) document that WebMAUS TextGrids were manually checked/corrected and that target-word boundaries were checked/corrected in Praat. Their production measures include f0, F1/F2 vowel-space measures, HNR, tempo, and sibilant spectral center-of-gravity/skewness.
- Current corpus access is contact-mediated, not a freely downloadable public deposit. Therefore the source stays in review rather than being admitted to core.

## Fresh discovery and screening

### HIGH review: SwehVd / Persson (2024), *The acoustic characteristics of Swedish vowels*

- Publication: Phonetica 81(6):599–643, DOI `10.1515/phon-2024-0011`.
- Public dataset identified by the paper: `https://osf.io/ruxnb/`; separate public analysis/code project: `https://osf.io/7uvj4/`.
- Primary controlled h-VOWEL-d speech production from 44 L1 Swedish talkers is reported in the study.
- The methods state that Montreal Forced Aligner word and segment boundaries were manually corrected by the author.
- Acoustic measurements include F1, F2, and F3 at five within-vowel timepoints (20%, 35%, 50%, 65%, 80%), supporting trajectory-preserving ingestion rather than midpoint reduction; vowel duration is also analyzed.
- The paper additionally reports checking formant estimates/outliers, making this a strong candidate for a human-verified production layer.
- Decision: HIGH review rather than immediate admission, because this run could verify the public repository URL from the article but could not yet enumerate the OSF file tree sufficiently to establish exactly which corrected boundaries/TextGrids, token-level raw-Hz trajectories, normalized values, speaker metadata, recording references, licenses, and missing-value conventions are directly downloadable.
- Added as a review increment at `data/registry/increments/2026-09-12_2311_review.csv`.

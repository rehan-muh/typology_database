Added `rescuespeech_2023` (RescueSpeech: A German Corpus for Speech Recognition in Search and Rescue Domain; Zenodo v2, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.8077622) to HIGH review.

Evidence screened from the authoritative Zenodo record:
- authentic German speech recorded from native speakers in simulated search-and-rescue exercises;
- record describes the speech as manually annotated;
- v2 ASR release contains 2412 clean utterances and an equal number of synthesized noisy counterparts;
- speech-enhancement material is explicitly augmentation-derived from clean utterances under multiple noise/SNR conditions;
- license: CC BY-NC 4.0.

Inventoried all three visible v2 files without mirroring bulk archives:
- `Readme.md`, 6.4 kB, MD5 `5b6bf9922c78e37aef96199f0eb34b4d`;
- `Task_ASR.tar.gz`, 643.2 MB, MD5 `c5b9f7857c1970bfe34eae0805c6987b`;
- `Task_enhancement.tar.gz`, 3.3 GB, MD5 `0a933817af7ccd56982225ae46c3722c`.

Decision: do not promote the deposit wholesale. Original clean SAR recordings are a potentially eligible primary lineage, but the meaning of “manually annotated” is not yet specific enough to establish manual phone/segment/prosodic intervals, landmarks, or manually verified production measurements. Synthesized noisy ASR counterparts, AudioSet noise, and enhancement augmentations are explicitly excluded from primary core and retained only as derivative lineage/reference.

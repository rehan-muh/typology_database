## Discovery

### CORPRES — A Fully Annotated Corpus of Russian Speech (`corpres_skrelin_2010`)
Status: **REVIEW — high priority**.

Primary Russian speech from 8 speakers (4 female, 4 male), 60 h total / 7.5 h per speaker, 528,458 running words. Published corpus description states that 40% was manually segmented and fully annotated on six levels: pitch marks, phonetic events, narrow phonetic transcription, wide phonetic transcription, orthographic transcription, and prosodic transcription. The other 60% is only partly annotated. This is strong substantive eligibility evidence, but this pass did not verify an authoritative publicly downloadable machine-readable corpus deposit or access/license terms. Therefore it is not admitted to core yet. If located, only tiers/portions with defensible human provenance should enter manual core.

Authoritative publication: https://aclanthology.org/L10-1188/ ; DOI 10.63317/4pvjxjnacnxx.

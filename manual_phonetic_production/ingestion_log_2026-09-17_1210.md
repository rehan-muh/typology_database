### Movement variability can be modulated in speech production (2022)
Primary production experiments. Published methods state that vowel onset was manually marked where periodicity/formants became visible and vowel offset where formants disappeared; trial-specific formant tracking errors were manually corrected by changing LPC order/pre-emphasis. This satisfies the manual-production criterion methodologically. Public observation-level files were not resolved in this pass, so no rows were fabricated or admitted.

### IFCASL

More than half of the corpus was manually corrected. The `Real` tier is the manually corrected realization tier; `Align` is forced-aligned canonical material. Core ingestion must isolate records actually corrected/verified and must not label the Align tier as manual.

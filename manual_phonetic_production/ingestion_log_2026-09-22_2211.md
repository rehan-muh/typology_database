## SAIT-EMA decision
Status: REVIEW, not core. Primary Mandarin production from 18 participants (12 native, 6 L2; 9F/9M), Carstens AG501 EMA at 250 Hz and synchronized mono audio at 48 kHz. Each speaker is documented as having 962 standard EMA items. Stimulus design: 575 monosyllables, 284 bisyllables, 103 sentences. Public dataset CC BY 4.0.

Critical inclusion check: paper explicitly states syllable initial/final TextGrids were automatically generated with Montreal Forced Aligner and warns of systematic temporal offsets, recommending manual verification/refinement for precise landmarks. Therefore deposited phonetic timing cannot be treated as manual or exhaustively human-verified. Session logs manually document erroneous-token re-recording and authors manually screened misaligned/unintentional repetitions, but this is recording QC, not evidence that the deposited segment boundaries were human verified. Automatic TextGrid boundaries must not enter core.

Potential future admissible layer: inspect Zenodo objects/session logs for isolatable manually created categorical production-error labels, manual token-QC judgments, or separately verified landmarks. Raw EMA/audio can only enter core if linked to a qualifying human annotation/measurement layer under project criteria.

Added `l2_arctic_manual_subset` to the review registry increment. The corpus documentation distinguishes the broad forced-aligned word/phone tiers from an approximately 150-utterance-per-speaker subset that was manually annotated with corrected word and phone boundaries and substitution/deletion/addition error tags. The candidate remains REVIEW rather than core because access requires acceptance of corpus license terms and a download form. Core admission requires actual file acquisition, file-level checksums, confirmation of which TextGrids belong to the manually corrected subset, and strict exclusion of forced-aligned-only observations from the manual core.

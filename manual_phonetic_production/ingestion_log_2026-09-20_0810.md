# Manual phonetic production ingestion — 2026-09-20 08:10 PT

## Discovery and admission

Added three UltraSuite source records with deliberately restricted admissible layers.

- `ultrasuite_uxtd` — Ultrax Typically Developing Children: 58 children (31F/27M, ages 5–12), primary English child speech with synchronized audio and ultrasound. The repository explicitly documents `reference_labels` manually revised by one annotator at speaker level (60 utterances) and word level (199 utterances). ACCEPT manual reference labels only.
- `ultrasuite_uxssd` — Ultrax Speech Sound Disorders: 8 children (2F/6M, ages 5–10), primary clinical child speech with synchronized audio and ultrasound. Shares the explicitly manually revised reference-label layer. ACCEPT manual reference labels only.
- `ultrasuite_upx` — UltraPhonix: 20 children (16M/4F, ages 6–13) with speech sound disorders, primary clinical speech plus ultrasound. Repository explicitly identifies `slt_labels` as manual annotations from the speech-language therapist when available. ACCEPT only that isolatable SLT layer pending exhaustive file enumeration.

UltraSuite as a whole is CC BY-NC 4.0. Authoritative data are available by rsync; the combined UXTD/UXSSD/UPX label package is approximately 80 MB, so this run registered the authoritative access path and a continuation backlog rather than copying large core media into GitHub.

## Strict exclusions inside accepted datasets

The general UXTD/UXSSD phone labels are explicitly force-aligned and not entirely manually revised. They are excluded even though manually revised word boundaries constrain them. General automatic word/phone alignments are excluded for UPX as well. No automatic boundaries were promoted simply because they coexist with manual layers.

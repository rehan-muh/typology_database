## Discovery / screening

Added **Phonetic Atlas of Germany (PAD)** to review. The corpus is primary elicited German-dialect production. Published documentation reports 30,422 narrow-transcribed tokens from 182 locations and about 3,800 phonetically specified allophones. Professional transcribers produced narrow transcriptions in 1980–1995, with independent transcriptions by two transcribers evaluated during supervision. A newer workflow creates WebMAUS TextGrids and then manually corrects them; a public project description reports 25 aligned recordings at that stage.

PAD is not core-admitted yet because this pass did not locate an authoritative public machine-readable deposit with explicit redistribution terms. It is a high-priority review candidate, with lineage explicitly separating the original narrow-transcription corpus from the newer corrected-alignment subset.

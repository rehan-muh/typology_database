## New accepted sources

### Tongue and Lips (TaL) Corpus — `tal_corpus_ultrasuite`
- Primary English production with synchronized audio, ultrasound tongue imaging, and lip video from 82 speakers (TaL1: 1 speaker/6 sessions; TaL80: 81 speakers/1 session each).
- Corpus paper/documentation reports ~24 h parallel multimodal data, ~13.5 h speech.
- Inclusion evidence is strong and specific: all spontaneous utterances were human-transcribed; long spontaneous utterances were manually segmented into shorter intervals, typically 5–10 s; `.lab` files provide start/end seconds and transcription.
- Retained raw modalities as future ingest targets: `.wav`, `.sync`, `.ult`, `.param`, `.mp4`, `.txt`, and spontaneous `.lab`.
- Seed dictionary records synchronization offset (`TimeInSecsOfFirstFrame`) so full ultrasound trajectories can later be aligned without reducing to midpoint values.
- Large binaries remain remote by design: TaL1 core ~49 GB; TaL80 core ~498 GB. Member-level rsync listing remains backlog.

### L2-ARCTIC v5.0 — `l2_arctic_v5`
- Primary non-native English production from 24 speakers across Arabic, Mandarin Chinese, Hindi, Korean, Spanish, and Vietnamese L1s.
- Manual read-speech layer: 3,599 utterances manually examined, with corrected word/phone boundaries and tagged phone errors (14,098 substitutions; 3,420 deletions; 1,092 additions).
- Manual spontaneous suitcase layer: 22 speakers, 26.1 min, ~3,083 word segments and ~9,458 phone segments; two trained research assistants divided transcription, cross-checked each other, then all transcriptions were checked by John Levis. Reported error annotations: 1,673 substitutions, 456 deletions, 90 additions.
- Strict layer separation recorded: `/annotation` manual TextGrids qualify; `/textgrid` forced-aligned TextGrids do not enter manual core.
- Seeded variables for words/phones/comments, interval boundaries, speaker/L1/gender, and primary waveform. Preserve raw ARPAbet/error tags and optional IPA comments; no inferred IPA.

# Ingestion log — 2026-09-23 21:11 PT

## Discovery

Accepted `spire_vcv_2021` (SPIRE VCV: An Acoustic-Articulatory Corpus with Three Different Speaking Rates). Authoritative SPIRE documentation establishes primary controlled non-singing production from 10 Indian/non-native English speakers, simultaneous audio and AG501 EMA, and manual VCV boundaries. Boundaries were annotated by a four-member team using wideband spectrograms, waveform, and Praat-derived glottal pulses; ambiguous cases were resolved by unanimous discussion. This satisfies the manual-annotation criterion directly.

Design: 5 vowels × 17 consonants, symmetric VCVs embedded in “Speak VCV today,” slow/normal/fast speaking rates, 3 repetitions per rate. Audio was recorded at 48 kHz and downsampled to 16 kHz. EMA was sampled at 250 Hz with six articulator sensors (upper lip, lower lip, jaw, tongue tip, tongue body, tongue dorsum), each in 3D; ear reference sensors support head-motion correction. Distribution page states CC-BY-4.0 but requires a form before corpus access.

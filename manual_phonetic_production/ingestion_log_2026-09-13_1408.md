## Fresh discovery and screening

### EXCLUDED — TunSwitch (`tunswitch_2023`)
Authoritative deposit: Zenodo record 8342762.

Screening result intentionally separates the two lineages/subsets:
- **TunSwitch TO:** primary elicited Tunisian production. The deposit describes participants reading provided phrases (89 participants; 2,631 distinct phrases), but does not establish that the relevant phonetic/transcription annotations for this primary subset were manually created, manually corrected, or exhaustively human-verified to the standard required for core admission.
- **TunSwitch CS:** strong manual transcript/code-switch annotation, but the underlying recordings come from radio shows and podcasts. The deposit additionally describes automatic WebRTC-VAD segmentation, overlap detection, and music filtering before annotation. This violates the primary-production / no-third-party-media rule.

Decision: **EXCLUDE** because no isolable subset simultaneously satisfies both primary-recording and required manual-annotation criteria.

### EXCLUDED — Hiemstra & Sadakata 2024 (`hiemstra_sadakata_2024_sts`)
Paper: *Sentences used in the speech-to-song illusion: Comparisons of acoustic vowel space*.

Strong annotation evidence:
- recordings manually segmented in Praat
- separate word, phoneme, and vowel TextGrid tiers
- vowel durations and F1/F2/F3 subsequently extracted

Exclusion reason:
- recordings were gathered from audiobooks and an audiobook-derived University of Amsterdam Music Cognition stimulus collection rather than collected as primary productions for the study.

Decision: **EXCLUDE — SECONDARY_MEDIA**, despite high-quality manual segmentation.

Both exclusions were appended to the central `data/registry/exclusions.csv` so later discovery passes do not repeatedly reconsider them as new sources.

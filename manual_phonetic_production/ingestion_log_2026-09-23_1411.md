### New accepted source: Golos (`golos_ru`)

Authoritative sources: Salute/Sber Golos repository, OpenSLR SLR114, and Karpov, Denisenko & Minkin (Interspeech 2021). The official repository and paper explicitly state that the recorded Russian audio was manually annotated on a crowdsourcing platform. This is primary non-singing production rather than media reuse or synthetic speech.

Known scale: Crowd train 979,796 utterances / 1,095 h; Farfield train 124,003 / 132.4 h; Crowd test 9,994 / 11.2 h; Farfield test 1,916 / 1.4 h. Total train = 1,103,799 utterances / 1,227.4 h; total test = 11,910 / 12.6 h.

Lineage rule: Crowd and Farfield are domains within one Golos lineage. Train/test partitions and archive shards are not independent datasets. QuartzNet and KenLM artifacts are derived models and explicitly excluded from production observations.

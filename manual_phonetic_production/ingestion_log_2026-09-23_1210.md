## Discovery

### ROG — review
Fresh 2026 LREC resource: *ROG: A Multi-Layer Manually Annotated Corpus of Spoken Slovenian*. The publication reports >75,000 words / 10 h, built from pre-existing Spoken Slovenian Treebank (SST) and newly available GOS 2 recordings. All speech has linguistic annotation; approximately half additionally has manual prosodic units, disfluencies, and dialogue acts. This is potentially valuable production/prosody material, but it is secondary reuse. Per database policy it remains **review**, not accepted, until the public deposit is opened and the genuinely new manual prosodic annotation files can be isolated and recording IDs lineage-matched against SST/GOS2. No inherited annotation is to be counted as newly ingested production data.

Evidence: https://aclanthology.org/2026.lrec-1.449/
